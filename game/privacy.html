HOW RIZO.GAME HANDLES DATA

Privacy notice

Plain-language notice: This page explains how the current Rizo.game website and local-save game operate.

Effective date: July 20, 2026

LOCAL GAME DATA

Rizo.game stores game progress, preferences, a randomly generated Keeper ID, install-prompt choices, and save backups on your device using browser storage. The base game does not require a name, email address, or account.

ADVERTISING

Advertising is disabled in the supplied launch build. If enabled, Google AdSense and/or H5 Games Ads may process device, cookie, approximate-location, interaction, and advertising information under Google's policies. A consent message may be shown where required. Rewarded ads are optional; ordinary display ads never require interaction.

EXTERNAL LINKS

Links to Rizo Apparel and other third-party services open separate websites with their own privacy practices.

CHILDREN

The configured experience is recommended for users age 13 and older and is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children under 13. Do not market the ad-supported version as child-directed without obtaining specialized legal and advertising-policy guidance.

CONTROLS

You may erase local game data from the in-game Journal or your browser settings. You may export a local save before doing so. Browser and consent-message settings may provide additional advertising controls.

CONTACT

Questions about this notice can be sent through the contact method published by Rizo Apparel at rizo.store.
