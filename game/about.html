RIZO.GAME
BY RIZO APPAREL
THE PLAYABLE WORLD OF RIZO APPAREL

A tiny flame with a whole life.

Rizo.game is a browser pet and collection game made by Rizo Apparel. You find a crying egg in a rainy forest, raise the creature inside, unlock lore, play repeatable arcade runs, collect rare Rizos, customize habitats, and try not to become the owner your pet complains about.

WHY IT EXISTS

The mascot came first. The game turns that Rizo flame into a living character and gives the clothing brand a playable world instead of a normal advertisement.

WHAT SAVES

Your pet, collection, inventory, story progress, and Keeper ID are stored locally in your browser. Use the Journal's export tool before clearing browser data or moving devices.

THE REAL-WORLD BRAND

Rizo Apparel makes the clothes behind the character. The game is free to play; real drops live at rizo.store.
